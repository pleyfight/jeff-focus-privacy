Jeff Focus privacy policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

The short version

This policy explains what the Jeff Focus app stores, where it's kept, and the rights and choices you have. In short, everything Jeff Focus saves stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no server and no tracking, and your Jeff Focus data is never sent to us.

Who we are

Jeff Focus is an iPhone app made by The Rejecktz (Micheal James Fergerson), an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at codingwithmjf@gmail.com.

What Jeff Focus stores on your iPhone

Jeff Focus saves only what it needs to work:

  • Your sessions: the name you give a session, its date and length, the mode you picked, how many times you tried to quit, how many mini-games you played, and how many apps, categories and websites were blocked.
  • Your settings: theme, default mode and length, your daily reminder and its time, and whether you've finished setup.
  • Your blocked list: the apps, categories and websites you choose in Apple's Screen Time picker. Apple hands these to Jeff Focus as private tokens, so Jeff Focus can't read their names or see what you do in those apps.
  • The session in progress, so closing the app doesn't reset your timer.
  • Timer readings: so changing the phone's clock can't end a session early, Jeff Focus reads the phone's built-in system clock and an ID that changes every time the phone restarts. Jeff Focus uses them only to measure time, and they never leave your phone.

What Jeff Focus doesn't collect

Jeff Focus doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or health data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites. Jeff Focus doesn't connect to the internet.

Some mini-games read how your phone is tilted while you play. Jeff Focus uses each reading in the moment and never saves it.

How the information is used

Jeff Focus uses what it stores to run the timer, block the apps you picked, show your session history, keep your settings and send your reminders. We don't use it to build a profile of you, show ads or make automated decisions about you.

Where privacy law asks for a legal reason, the reason is that this information is needed to provide the app you chose to use.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or give it to anyone. Jeff Focus doesn't send it to us either.

Jeff Focus relies on built-in iPhone features to work. Apple's Screen Time applies the blocks you choose, and iOS shows Jeff Focus's notifications. Those notifications are created on your phone, not sent from a server.

Storage and security

Jeff Focus keeps its data in its own private storage on your iPhone, which iOS protects with your passcode. Because the data stays on your phone, it isn't transferred to other countries by us.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the backup may include Jeff Focus's data. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

Jeff Focus writes basic status messages to the iPhone's system log to help with troubleshooting. They stay on your phone unless you choose to share analytics with Apple in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. If you also turn on "Share With App Developers" there, Apple may send us crash reports for Jeff Focus. We use those reports only to fix bugs.

How long data is kept

Jeff Focus keeps your data on your phone until you delete it or remove the app. We keep any crash reports Apple sends us only as long as we need them to fix the problem.

Your choices

  • Clear session history (Jeff Focus Settings) deletes your past sessions and keeps your settings and blocked list.
  • Delete all data (Jeff Focus Settings) deletes your history, blocked list, settings and Jeff Focus's notifications, then takes you back to setup. It can't be used while a Jeff Focus session is running.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone.
  • You can turn off Screen Time access or notifications for Jeff Focus at any time in the iPhone's Settings app. This withdraws your permission for those features.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or limit how it's used, and to withdraw consent.

Because your Jeff Focus data stays on your phone and never reaches us, you can use these rights directly with the controls above. If you have a question about your data or want to make a request, email codingwithmjf@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days.

  • European Economic Area and United Kingdom: you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California: we don't sell or share personal information, and we don't collect sensitive personal information. We won't treat you differently for using your privacy rights.
  • Japan: we don't acquire personal information through Jeff Focus and don't provide it to third parties. You can send questions or complaints to the email above.

Children

Jeff Focus is a general audience app. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll also mention it in the app's update notes.
